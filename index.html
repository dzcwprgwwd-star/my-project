Our Story

It started with a backyard griddle

In 2016, two friends with one cast-iron press set out to make the perfect smash burger. Ten years and one tiny food truck later, Smash & Stack is the neighborhood spot people line up for — but the obsession is exactly the same: great beef, smashed hard, served fast.

  • Ground fresh, in-houseChuck & brisket blend ground every morning — never frozen.
  • Buns baked locallyBrioche delivered fresh daily from a family bakery two blocks away.
  • Sauces from scratchEvery drip of house sauce and aioli is made in our kitchen.
How We Cook

Three steps to a perfect smash

Ball & sear

A loose ball of fresh beef hits the screaming-hot flat-top and gets pressed paper-thin.

Crisp the edges

That contact creates the Maillard crust — the lacy, caramelized edge that makes a smash burger sing.

Stack & serve

Cheese melts over the top, everything stacks on a buttered brioche bun, and it's in your hands in minutes.
